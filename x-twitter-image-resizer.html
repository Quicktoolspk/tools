Overview

X (Twitter) Image Resizer Online

Prepare images for X (formerly Twitter) with the right dimensions for your profile header and posts. QuickToolsPK's X Image Resizer is designed to help you select a target image size, adjust the framing, and prepare graphics for publishing.

Whether you are sharing a personal update, promoting a business, or updating your profile banner, choosing the right dimensions helps keep your visual content clear and well-presented.

Benefits

Why use an X image resizer?

Images created for one platform may not fit another platform's layout. Resizing an image to the intended dimensions can help you avoid unwanted cropping, distorted proportions, and poorly positioned text.

An X image resizer can help you:

  • Prepare a 1500 × 500 px profile header.
  • Resize landscape, square, and portrait post graphics.
  • Keep logos, faces, and headlines positioned correctly.
  • Crop an image to fill a frame or fit the complete image with padding.
  • Create consistent graphics for personal branding and business posts.
Step by step

How to resize an image for X

  1. Upload your image: Select the image you want to prepare.
  2. Choose a preset: Select the header, landscape post, square post, or another available format.
  3. Adjust the framing: Crop the image to fill the canvas or fit the complete image within it.
  4. Preview your result: Check that important details and text remain visible.
  5. Download the resized image: Export the finished image in a supported format and check it in X before publishing.
Dimensions

What is the best size for an X header?

The recommended X header size is 1500 × 500 pixels, with a 3:1 aspect ratio. Because the visible area can vary by device and browser, avoid placing important content near the top and bottom edges. The profile picture can also overlap part of the header.

Dimensions

What is the best size for an X post image?

X does not prescribe one universal pixel size for every standard post image. A 1600 × 900 px landscape canvas is a practical choice, while square and portrait formats can suit other types of content. X's guidance says a single image between 2:1 and 3:4 can display in full in the timeline. Always review the post preview before publishing.

Compare

Crop or fit: which should you choose?

Crop to fill

Works well when the image needs to cover the entire canvas. However, the crop may remove some content around the edges.

Fit with padding

Keeps the complete image visible inside the selected dimensions. Depending on the original aspect ratio, this can leave empty space around the image. A background colour can help the result look intentional.

Choose the option that best preserves the subject and any essential text.
Best practices

Tips for better X images

  • Use high-resolution source images when possible.
  • Keep text readable at smaller display sizes.
  • Avoid stretching images because it can distort faces, logos, and objects.
  • Check headers on both desktop and mobile.
  • Keep important content away from the edges.
  • Verify the exported dimensions and file format before uploading.
Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the X header image size?
X recommends 1500 × 500 pixels. Some displays may crop around 60 pixels from the top and bottom, so keep important elements within the central area.
Can I use the same image for a header and a post?
You can, but the dimensions differ. A header uses a wide 3:1 layout, while a post may use landscape, square, or portrait proportions. You may need separate versions to preserve the full design.
Why does X crop my post image?
X's timeline display depends on the image's aspect ratio and the post layout. Images outside the supported full-display range may appear cropped in the preview. Check the post composer before publishing.
Should I crop or fit an image?
Crop when you want the canvas filled edge to edge. Fit when preserving the whole image is more important than filling the canvas.
Does resizing improve image quality?
Resizing can make an image fit the required dimensions, but it cannot restore detail missing from a low-resolution original. Avoid enlarging small images excessively.
Is this tool free?
If you offer the tool free of charge, you can state that users can resize images without paying. Confirm any usage limits or other restrictions before publishing this claim.
