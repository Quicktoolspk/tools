How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Add your JPG images. Drag and drop files onto the upload area, or click Select JPG files to browse. You can add multiple images at once.
  2. Review the previews. Each selected image appears as a thumbnail. Click the red × on a thumbnail to remove it.
  3. Set the quality. Move the slider between 0% and 100%. Higher values preserve more detail but produce a larger PDF.
  4. Convert and download. Click Convert to PDF. The PDF downloads automatically with one image per page, scaled to fit.

Why convert images to PDF

  • Consistent sharing. A PDF keeps every page together and displays the same on any device.
  • Printing and archiving. PDFs are widely accepted for printing, scanning archives, and official submissions.
  • Organization. Combine multiple photos or scanned pages into one tidy file instead of many separate images.
  • Easy sharing. A single PDF is simpler to email or attach than a folder of JPGs.

Frequently asked questions

Is this JPG to PDF converter free?
Yes. The tool is free to use and does not require an account or login.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. The images are read locally and the PDF is generated in your browser with jsPDF. Nothing is sent to a server.
Can I add multiple images to one PDF?
Yes. Select or drop several JPG files at once. Each one becomes a page in the resulting PDF, in the order they appear in the preview list.
Which image formats are supported?
This tool accepts JPG and JPEG files. To convert PNG or WEBP images, use the Image Format Converter first.
Does the quality slider change the PDF file size?
Yes. Higher quality values generally produce larger PDFs, while lower values reduce size at the cost of some image detail.
Will the PDF work on mobile devices?
Yes. The generated PDF is a standard PDF file that opens in any PDF reader on desktop, tablet, or mobile.
