Step by step

How to Make a Passport Size Photo

Follow these steps as a general workflow. The exact controls depend on the features available in the published tool.

  1. Choose a clear photo. Use a recent, sharp image with your face fully visible and even lighting.
  2. Open the Passport Size Photo Maker. Use the upload option provided in the tool interface.
  3. Select the required dimensions. Choose a supported preset or enter the required dimensions if custom sizing is available.
  4. Adjust the framing if supported. Make sure your head and face are positioned according to the relevant official guidelines.
  5. Review the result. Check the dimensions, image orientation, background, sharpness and file specifications.
  6. Export your photo. Use the available download or export option, if supported, and confirm that the saved file meets your application requirements.
Important: Some official processes prohibit digital alteration of passport photos, even if an edited image looks correct. For example, the US Department of State says passport photos should be original and not digitally changed. Check the rules for your specific application before editing your image.
Use cases

What Can You Use a Passport Photo Maker For?

  • Passport applications: Prepare an image for the relevant passport photo specifications.
  • Visa applications: Check the required dimensions and digital image rules for your destination.
  • ID cards: Prepare a photo for an identification card when the issuing organization allows it.

Requirements differ by application. Using a photo maker does not guarantee that a government agency, embassy, employer or institution will accept the result.

Best practices

Tips for Preparing a Better Photo

  • Use even lighting: Avoid harsh shadows on your face or behind your head.
  • Face the camera: Follow the head position and facial expression rules for your application.
  • Keep the image sharp: Avoid blurry, grainy or heavily compressed photos.
  • Check the background: Use the background color and appearance required by the relevant authority.
  • Avoid unnecessary edits: Do not use filters, retouching or AI changes if the application requires an unaltered photograph.
  • Check the saved file: Confirm the dimensions, orientation, file format and size before uploading or printing.
  • Verify the official rules: Requirements may differ even between applications in the same country.
Troubleshooting

Common Passport Photo Problems

The photo is the wrong size

Check the exact width and height required for your application. Do not assume that a generic passport-size preset is suitable for every country.

The face looks too large or too small

Review the official head-size and framing rules. Correct overall image dimensions do not automatically mean that the face is correctly positioned.

The image looks blurry

Start with a higher-quality original photo. Resizing a low-resolution image cannot restore missing detail.

The background does not meet the requirements

Check whether the receiving authority requires a plain light-colored, white or another specified background. Do not digitally change the background if edits are prohibited.

The upload is rejected

Read the rejection message and compare the image with the official requirements. Check file type, pixel dimensions, file size, orientation and photo composition.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a passport size photo maker?
A passport size photo maker is a tool intended to help prepare an image for passport photo dimensions. Depending on the tool, it may support resizing, cropping or other photo-preparation options.
Can I make a visa size photo online?
You can prepare a visa photo using a suitable photo tool, provided its available options meet your application's requirements. Always check the destination authority's current instructions.
What is the standard passport photo size?
There is no universal standard for all countries and applications. For example, a US printed passport photo is generally 2 × 2 inches, while a UK printed passport photo is 35 × 45 mm.
Can I use the same photo for every country?
Not necessarily. Countries and application types may require different dimensions, facial framing, backgrounds, file formats or image-editing rules.
Can I use a photo taken with my phone?
A phone photo may be suitable if it meets the specific requirements for your application. Check image clarity, lighting, background, framing and file specifications.
Does resizing guarantee that my photo will be accepted?
No. Correct dimensions are only one part of photo compliance. The receiving authority makes the final decision, and other rules may apply.
Can I change the background or retouch my face?
Only if the rules for your particular application allow it. Some official passport and visa processes restrict digital alterations. Check the relevant authority's guidance before editing.
What should I do if my photo is rejected?
Review the rejection reason and the official photo guidelines. You may need to take a new photo with better lighting, framing or background, or provide a file with the correct dimensions and format.
