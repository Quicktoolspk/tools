Anthropic IPO: When Will Anthropic Go Public? Stock Price, IPO Date & How to Invest

November 18, 2026 6 min read By QuickToolsPK Finance

Anthropic is one of the biggest names in artificial intelligence, and investors are increasingly searching for Anthropic IPO, Anthropic stock, ANT stock, IPO price, and when Anthropic will go public.

The company behind Claude AI has attracted significant attention from investors because of its AI models, enterprise products, and competition with OpenAI, Google, and other AI companies.

But one major question remains: Can you buy Anthropic stock right now, and has the company announced an IPO date?

Below is what investors need to know about Anthropic's potential IPO, its stock ticker, expected valuation, IPO price, and ways to gain exposure before it becomes publicly traded.

Direct Answer: The Anthropic IPO is scheduled for November 19, 2026, with the company expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under ticker symbol ANT. The IPO pricing is expected to be finalized on November 18, 2026, ahead of the first day of trading.

What Is Anthropic and Why Is Its IPO So Important?

Anthropic is an artificial intelligence company founded by former OpenAI employees. It develops AI models and products under the Claude brand, including AI assistants, coding tools, enterprise solutions and APIs for businesses and developers.

The company competes in the rapidly growing AI market against OpenAI, Google, Meta, Microsoft and other technology companies.

Anthropic has attracted investor interest because of its:

  • Claude AI family of models.
  • AI coding and developer tools.
  • Enterprise AI adoption.
  • AI research and model development.
  • Large investments in computing infrastructure.
  • Partnerships with major cloud and technology companies.

The IPO is attracting particular attention because investors are trying to determine how much long-term value the AI industry can generate and whether current valuations can be supported by future revenue and profits.

Is Anthropic Stock a Good Investment?

Anthropic could be an interesting long-term AI investment, but its IPO is also likely to carry significant risk.

Potential Advantages

  • AI Growth: AI is being adopted across industries.
  • Claude Adoption: Competing in AI assistant and coding markets.
  • Enterprise Demand: Businesses increasingly exploring AI automation.
  • Visibility & Liquidity: A public listing could provide greater visibility.

Risks to Consider
