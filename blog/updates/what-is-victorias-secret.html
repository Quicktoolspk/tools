What Is Victoria's Secret? Brand History, Products & What It's Known For

Updated: November 2026 6 min read Brand Guide

An introduction to Victoria's Secret, its products and global fashion brand identity.

Victoria's Secret is a well-known American fashion and beauty brand, best recognised for its lingerie, bras, panties, sleepwear, fragrances and body-care products. Over the decades, the name has become closely associated with glamorous fashion shows, signature fragrances and a distinctive retail identity.

The brand is part of Victoria's Secret & Co., a publicly traded retail company. Its product range goes beyond lingerie and includes casual clothing, lounge sets, activewear, swimwear and beauty products.

In this guide, you will learn what Victoria's Secret is, how the brand started, what products it sells, what it is famous for, and the answers to some of the most frequently asked questions about the company.

What Is Victoria's Secret?

Victoria's Secret is an American fashion and beauty retailer that is best known for its lingerie, bras, panties, sleepwear, fragrances and body-care products. The brand operates through a mix of physical stores and online channels, and it has become one of the most recognisable names in intimate apparel.

The company behind the brand, Victoria's Secret & Co., is a publicly traded business that also owns the PINK brand and Adore Me. Its product catalogue covers everyday essentials as well as more elaborate collections and seasonal ranges.

For many shoppers, the name Victoria's Secret is associated with premium-feeling products, glamorous marketing and the famous fashion show that helped turn the brand into a global pop-culture reference.

The History of Victoria's Secret

Victoria's Secret was founded in 1977 in San Francisco by Roy Raymond. The original idea was to create a lingerie shopping experience that felt more comfortable and welcoming than what was typically available at the time.

The business later grew into an internationally recognised name in intimate apparel and fashion. Its glamorous stores, distinctive branding, advertising campaigns and fashion shows helped it become widely known around the world.

Today, Victoria's Secret operates as an independent public company. It separated from L Brands in August 2021, when the Victoria's Secret business became Victoria's Secret & Co.

Victoria's Secret is often written as "victoria secret" in search engines. Both spellings refer to the same brand, and the correct official spelling is Victoria's Secret.

What Products Does Victoria's Secret Sell?

Victoria's Secret offers several categories of products. The main ones include:

  1. Bras: Different styles, including everyday bras, push-up bras, wireless bras and sports bras.
  2. Panties: A wide range of styles, cuts, fabrics and designs.
  3. Lingerie: Fashion-inspired lingerie, sleepwear and special-occasion collections.
  4. Fragrances: Perfumes, body mists and scented products.
  5. Body Care: Lotions, body washes and other personal-care products.
  6. Sleepwear and Loungewear: Pajamas, robes and comfortable clothing.
  7. Activewear and Swimwear: Selected collections for exercise, relaxation and swimming.

These categories reflect the company's current product range and show how the brand has expanded beyond its original focus on lingerie.

What Is Victoria's Secret Famous For?

Victoria's Secret is particularly famous for its lingerie collections, signature fragrances, glamorous advertising and annual fashion show.

The Victoria's Secret Fashion Show became a major fashion and entertainment event, featuring runway models, elaborate outfits, wings and musical performances. The original show debuted in 1995. After being cancelled in 2019, the company announced its return in 2024 with a refreshed approach.

The fashion show helped turn the brand into a global pop-culture phenomenon and introduced several of its models to mainstream audiences.

What Is PINK by Victoria's Secret?

PINK is a lifestyle brand associated with Victoria's Secret & Co. It focuses on a younger audience and offers casual clothing, loungewear, activewear, bras, panties, accessories and beauty products.

Although PINK and Victoria's Secret are closely connected, they have different branding and product identities. PINK tends to have a more casual, campus-oriented style, while Victoria's Secret focuses on lingerie, beauty and fashion collections.

Is Victoria's Secret a Luxury Brand?

Victoria's Secret is often associated with premium, glamorous fashion and beauty products. However, it is more accurately described as a major specialty retail brand rather than an exclusively high-fashion luxury house.

Its collections cover different styles and price points, from everyday essentials to more elaborate lingerie and fragrance products. This broad range is one reason the brand appeals to a wide variety of shoppers.

Is Victoria's Secret Available Worldwide?

Victoria's Secret has an international retail and online presence. The company reports operations across approximately 70 countries through its stores and international business channels.

Product availability, shipping options and prices vary by country. Shoppers should check the official Victoria's Secret website for their region to confirm current availability and delivery details.

Final Thoughts

Victoria's Secret is one of the most recognisable names in lingerie and beauty retail. From its founding in 1977 to its current position as part of Victoria's Secret & Co., the brand has built a global identity around fashion, fragrance and glamorous marketing.

Whether you are researching the brand for the first time or checking a specific detail, the key facts are straightforward: Victoria's Secret is an American fashion and beauty retailer, founded in San Francisco in 1977, and best known for its lingerie, fragrances and iconic fashion shows.
