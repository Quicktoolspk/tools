Manni L. Perez: The Breakthrough Actress Bringing Lucia to Life in GTA VI

November 17, 2026 5 min read By QuickToolsPK Entertainment

When Rockstar Games revealed the first official trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI, it didn't just break internet viewing records; it ignited a massive community investigation to identify the actors playing the game's protagonists, Jason and Lucia. At the center of that global conversation is Manni L. Perez, the talented actress whose performance capture and voice work breathe life into Lucia, one of the franchise's most anticipated lead characters.

Direct Answer: Manni L. Perez is a professional actress and voice performer who stars as Lucia Caminos in Grand Theft Auto VI. Following years of fan sleuthing and official announcements, her breakout role earned her widespread acclaim, including a nomination for Best Performance at The Game Awards.

From Fan Theories to Official Recognition

For months following the initial GTA VI trailer release, gaming sleuths scoured voice samples, facial structures, and career resumes to match the digital characters to real-world talent. Community comparisons quickly centered on Manni L. Perez due to her vocal cadence, physical structure, and previous acting background.

That speculation culminated when Rockstar Games and subsequent industry accolades confirmed her pivotal role as Lucia. Her nomination for Best Performance at The Game Awards validated the depth, emotional grit, and physical dedication required for performance capture in a multi-year AAA blockbuster development cycle.

Prior Experience With Rockstar Games

Interestingly, Grand Theft Auto VI is not Perez's first encounter with the sprawling crime franchise. Long before landing the co-lead role as Lucia, she worked on smaller background and supporting audio roles within the Rockstar ecosystem:

  • GTA Online: Voiced a casino blackjack dealer.
  • Grand Theft Auto V: Credited as a background character ("Clubber #2").

Transitioning from bit parts in GTA V and GTA Online to heading up the next-generation installment as a primary protagonist marks one of the most remarkable career leaps in modern video game history.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who plays Lucia in Grand Theft Auto VI?
Lucia in Grand Theft Auto VI is portrayed by actress and performance-capture artist Manni L. Perez.
2. What other games has Manni L. Perez appeared in?
Aside from her leading role in GTA VI, Perez has minor voice credits within the Rockstar universe, including voicing a blackjack dealer in GTA Online and a background role in GTA V.
3. Has Manni L. Perez won or been nominated for any awards for GTA VI?
Yes. Her performance as Lucia earned her a nomination for Best Performance at The Game Awards.
4. What TV shows has Manni L. Perez acted in?
Perez has appeared as a guest star in several hit television series, such as Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Chicago P.D., The Blacklist, Blindspot, and Marvel's Jessica Jones.

Conclusion

From bit parts in Los Santos to starring in one of the most anticipated games of all time, Manni L. Perez's journey is a testament to persistence and talent. As the world prepares for the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, her portrayal of Lucia is already cementing her legacy in the gaming industry.
