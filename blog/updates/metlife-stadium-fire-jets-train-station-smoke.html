MetLife Stadium Fire: What Happened Near the Jets Train Station?

November 16, 2026 6 min read Developing
Breaking: A fire broke out in an unoccupied postgame tent outside MetLife Stadium after the Jets-Bills game. The stadium itself was not on fire. No injuries reported. NJ Transit service unaffected.

Firefighters responded to a tent fire outside MetLife Stadium on November 15, 2026. (Illustrative image)

A large fire broke out outside MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on Sunday evening, November 15, 2026, following the NFL game between the New York Jets and Buffalo Bills.

Thick black smoke and visible flames attracted attention around the stadium, with videos circulating online showing smoke rising near the adjacent train station and drifting toward the stadium. The incident raised questions among fans and people watching the videos: Was MetLife Stadium actually on fire? Did the train station catch fire? Were passengers or stadium visitors injured?

According to the New Jersey State Police, the fire started in an unoccupied postgame tent near Lot A, next to the American Express (AMEX) gate. Firefighters responded and extinguished the blaze. Officials reported no injuries and no damage to other structures.

Key Clarification: The stadium itself did not catch fire. The fire was outside the venue, although smoke reached areas around and inside the stadium.

What Happened at MetLife Stadium on November 15, 2026?

The incident happened after the Buffalo Bills defeated the New York Jets in their Week 10 NFL matchup at MetLife Stadium.

The game ended with the Bills winning 31-12. Later that evening, emergency responders received a report of smoke outside the stadium.

New Jersey State Police said troopers responded at approximately 5:51 p.m. ET near Lot A, next to the AMEX gate. When they arrived, they saw visible flames coming from a postgame tent.

The tent was unoccupied when the fire started.

Firefighters from the New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority, Moonachie, and Carlstadt responded and extinguished the blaze. East Rutherford Fire Department personnel also provided mutual aid.

Why Did People Think MetLife Stadium Was on Fire?

Videos of the incident created confusion because the smoke appeared close to the stadium and could be seen over parts of the venue.

Some social media posts described the situation as MetLife Stadium being on fire. However, official information clarified that the fire was in a tent outside the stadium.

MetLife Stadium Train Station Fire: Was the Meadowlands Station Burning?

One of the most searched questions after the incident was whether the fire happened inside the MetLife Stadium train station.

The nearby rail stop is Meadowlands Station, which serves the Meadowlands Sports Complex and provides transportation for stadium events.

Although some eyewitness reports and social media posts described a fire near the train station, the New Jersey State Police identified the burning structure as a postgame tent near Lot A and the AMEX gate.

Did NJ Transit Train Service Stop?

Reports citing an NJ Transit spokesperson said the fire did not affect nearby train service.

This was important for fans who had attended the Jets-Bills game and were concerned about leaving the stadium or using the Meadowlands rail connection.

Good news for commuters: There was no confirmed report in the sources reviewed that the fire caused a train-service shutdown.

What Caused the Fire Near MetLife Stadium?

As of November 16, 2026, officials had not publicly confirmed the cause of the fire.

The New Jersey State Police said the cause was under investigation and that no additional information was available at the time of their statement.

There is currently no verified evidence establishing whether the fire resulted from an electrical problem, equipment malfunction, heating equipment, or another source.

It is important not to treat speculation on social media as an official explanation. Further information may become available if investigators release additional findings.

Were Anyone Injured in the MetLife Stadium Fire?

No injuries were reported by New Jersey State Police.

Officials also said the tent was unoccupied when the fire started. This meant there were no reported occupants inside the burning structure.

Additionally, no damage was reported to other structures.

Although videos showed significant flames and smoke, the confirmed reports did not describe injuries or damage to the stadium itself.

How Did Firefighters Respond?

Emergency crews responded after state troopers received the smoke report at approximately 5:51 p.m.

The response involved:

  • New Jersey State Police troopers.
  • New Jersey Sports and Exposition Authority fire personnel.
  • Moonachie fire personnel.
  • Carlstadt fire personnel.
  • East Rutherford Fire Department mutual aid.

The fire was extinguished, and officials reported no damage to additional structures or injuries at the scene.

Conclusion

The MetLife Stadium fire on November 15, 2026, attracted widespread attention after thick smoke and flames appeared near the stadium following the Jets-Bills game.

Despite early confusion online, the stadium itself did not catch fire. New Jersey State Police confirmed that an unoccupied postgame tent near Lot A and the AMEX gate was burning. Firefighters extinguished the blaze, with no injuries or damage to additional structures reported.

The fire also raised questions about the nearby Meadowlands train station, but reports indicated that NJ Transit service was not affected. The cause of the incident remained under investigation as of November 16.

This article reflects reporting available on November 16, 2026. The cause of the fire is not confirmed, and the article should be updated if police, the stadium, or NJ Transit release new information.
