Florida Learjet 60 Crash: Nathalia Gadelha, Upper Jets Connection & Key Details

Updated: November 2, 2026 8 min read ● NTSB Inquiry Ongoing
Breaking: A Bombardier Learjet 60 (Reg: N722BD) crashed in Greenacres, Florida on November 1, 2026. All 9 people aboard died. The NTSB is leading the investigation.

A private Bombardier Learjet 60 crashed in Greenacres, Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, November 1, 2026. All nine people aboard lost their lives when the aircraft crashed near South Military Trail while en route from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas.

The incident has drawn international focus due to dramatic dashcam recordings, emergency air traffic control audio, and the early identification of the young co-pilot. Both the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) have launched an investigation into the probable causes.

One of the victims was identified by her family as 20-year-old co-pilot Nathalia Gadelha Filgueiras. A resident of Boca Raton, Florida, Nathalia was widely recognized as a rising talent in civil aviation.

  • Early Achievements: Earned her Private Pilot License (PPL) at just 17 years old in 2023 — before obtaining her driver's license.
  • Education: Attended West Boca Raton High School and completed flight coursework via the Palm Beach County School District's Aviation Academy and American Flyers flight school.
  • Career Goals: Had accumulated hundreds of flight hours with the ultimate goal of becoming a commercial airline pilot.

Flight Trajectory & Final Air Traffic Control Logs

The aircraft departed Austin, Texas, before encountering severe atmospheric conditions over Florida. Air traffic control (ATC) voice logs reveal critical details regarding the emergency:

1 Weather Reports: The crew reported icing conditions, precipitation, and turbulence, requesting permission to climb to a higher altitude.
2 Emergency Declaration: Shortly after, the crew declared an emergency, reporting an uncontrolled rapid rate of descent exceeding 10,000 feet per minute alongside overspeed alerts.
3 Impact: The aircraft crashed near Military Trail and 10th Avenue North in Greenacres, creating a major fireball and impact crater near a residential trailer park. No casualties were reported on the ground.

Corporate Connections: Upper Jets & Aircraft Registration

  • Reported Operator/Affiliation: Early media reports linked co-pilot Nathalia Gadelha to Upper Jets, a Fort Lauderdale-based charter broker and aircraft operator.
  • FAA Records: Official registry records list the Learjet 60 under a Delaware-based entity (LR60271 LLC).
  • Investigative Scope: The NTSB is reviewing structural, operational, and maintenance logs to confirm operator compliance and maintenance standards.
At this stage, media coverage linking Upper Jets to the aircraft does not establish legal responsibility for the crash. Conclusions about operator liability should only be drawn from the NTSB's official findings.

Key Areas of the NTSB Investigation

Investigators are evaluating five primary technical categories:

1 Meteorological Data: Evaluating localized thunderstorm cells, icing risks, and severe wind gusts present at the time.
2 Flight Data Recorders (Black Box): Retrieving Cockpit Voice Recorders (CVR) and Flight Data Recorders (FDR).
3 Airframe & Propulsion: Examining engine remains for structural integrity or failure prior to impact.
4 Maintenance History: Reviewing complete airworthiness logs and inspection records.
5 Crew Communication: Analyzing cockpit audio dynamics during the emergency descent.
Important: None of these areas has been identified as the confirmed cause. Only the NTSB's final determination can establish what happened. Early reporting should not be treated as an official conclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Was Nathalia Gadelha Filgueiras certified to fly a Learjet 60?
Nathalia was serving as the co-pilot aboard the flight. She had amassed over 1,300 flight hours and held advanced multi-engine ratings.
2. Have all victims' names been officially released?
While family members confirmed Nathalia's presence, official authorities have withheld the full passenger manifest pending complete family notifications.
3. What caused the Florida Learjet 60 crash?
The official cause has not been determined. Severe weather, icing, technical anomalies, and control loss are currently being examined by the NTSB.
