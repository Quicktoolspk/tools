Jeffery Simmons Trade: Chicago Bears Acquire Titans Star in Blockbuster Deal

Last checked: October 29, 2026 8 min read Developing Story

Jeffery Simmons during his time with the Tennessee Titans. (Illustrative image)

The Chicago Bears are making a major move ahead of the NFL trade deadline, reportedly acquiring star defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons from the Tennessee Titans in a deal involving two first-round draft picks. The move gives Chicago a proven interior defender as the team looks to strengthen its roster, while Tennessee receives valuable draft capital for the future.

The reported deal has quickly become a major topic in NFL news because of Simmons' production, his recently signed contract extension, and the price Chicago is paying to bring him in.

In this guide, we break down the reported trade terms, Simmons' age and career statistics, his contract, how he could help the Bears, and what the move means for both Chicago and Tennessee.

Jeffery Simmons trade: What happened?

According to reporting from NFL.com and the Associated Press, the Bears are acquiring Simmons and a 2028 fifth-round draft pick from the Titans. In return, Tennessee is set to receive Chicago's 2027 and 2028 first-round picks and a 2027 fourth-round pick.

Team Reported Return
Chicago Bears DT Jeffery Simmons; 2028 fifth-round pick
Tennessee Titans 2027 first-round pick; 2028 first-round pick; 2027 fourth-round pick

The deal represents a significant investment by Chicago. First-round picks are among the most valuable assets a team can use to add young talent, so giving up two of them signals a willingness to prioritize immediate roster improvement.

Important status note: This article is based on reporting available on October 29, 2026. Some initial reports said the deal was pending a physical and had not yet been formally announced by the teams. Readers should check the latest official team updates for any changes to its status.

Why did the Bears trade for Jeffery Simmons?

The Bears appear to be investing in a player who can make an impact from the middle of the defensive line. A disruptive interior defender can pressure the quarterback, challenge running plays, and make it more difficult for opposing offenses to operate comfortably.

Simmons is not an unproven prospect. He is a four-time Pro Bowl selection who earned first-team All-Pro recognition in 2025 after recording a career-high 11 sacks. Those accomplishments help explain why he commanded such a substantial trade package.

For Chicago, the potential benefits include:

  • Interior pass rush: Simmons can generate pressure without relying exclusively on edge rushers.
  • Run defense: His size and production make him a significant presence in the middle of the defensive line.
  • Experience: He brings multiple seasons of NFL experience and a record of high-level individual honors.
  • Immediate impact: Unlike a draft pick, Simmons already has a substantial professional track record.

These are potential football benefits, not a guarantee of future wins. The final impact will depend on his health, scheme fit, teammates, and performance after the move.

Who is Jeffery Simmons?

Jeffery Simmons is an NFL defensive tackle who entered the league with the Tennessee Titans after being selected 19th overall in the 2019 NFL Draft. He played college football at Mississippi State and developed into one of Tennessee's most prominent defensive players.

How old is Jeffery Simmons?

Jeffery Simmons is listed as 29 years old on the Tennessee Titans' player profile. He was drafted in 2019 and has accumulated multiple seasons of NFL experience.

His age matters when evaluating the trade because Chicago is not simply acquiring a young developmental player. It is investing in an established defensive lineman who has already produced at a high level. How much value the Bears receive over the coming seasons will depend on his health and continued performance.

Jeffery Simmons stats: 2026 season and career

Simmons' 2025 season was a career-best year for sacks, and he continued to produce during the 2026 season before the reported trade.

2026 regular-season stats

Through seven games, the Titans' official career statistics list Simmons with 38 total tackles and four sacks. Pro Football Focus also listed 24 total pressures and a 76.7 overall defensive grade in its October 28, 2026 player update. PFF grades are its own evaluation system and should not be confused with official NFL box-score statistics.

2025 season

Simmons recorded a career-high 11 sacks in 2025, along with 67 tackles, 17 tackles for loss, 60 quarterback pressures, and three forced fumbles, according to the Titans' reporting on his season. His performance helped him earn first-team All-Pro recognition.

Career production

Before the reported move, Reuters reported that Simmons had recorded 46.5 career sacks and 414 tackles across 106 NFL games. These figures illustrate why he is viewed as a proven player rather than a speculative trade target.

Jeffery Simmons contract: How much is he making?

In June 2026, the Titans announced a multi-year contract extension with Simmons. NFL.com reported the extension at three years and $105.8 million, including $100 million guaranteed. The contract made him the highest-paid defensive tackle by average annual value at the time of the agreement, according to NFL Network's reporting.

That contract is an important part of the trade discussion. Chicago is not only giving up valuable draft picks; it is also taking on a major financial commitment to an established defensive star.

For Tennessee, trading a player soon after agreeing to a large extension is a notable change in direction. For Chicago, the calculation is whether Simmons' impact on the field justifies both the draft cost and the salary commitment.

Contract figures can be reported differently depending on whether a source includes guarantees, incentives, or the full value of an extension. The $105.8 million and $100 million guaranteed figures above reflect the cited June reporting; they should not be presented as Simmons' cash earnings in a single season.

Is Jeffery Simmons good? What do his numbers say?

Simmons has a strong statistical and awards record. His four Pro Bowl selections and 2025 first-team All-Pro honor provide evidence of sustained recognition at a high level. His 11 sacks in 2025 also show that his impact can extend beyond stopping the run.

The 2026 PFF update available before the trade listed him 12th among 126 qualified interior defensive linemen by overall grade and fifth by pass-rush grade. These rankings provide another useful perspective, although PFF's grading methodology is independent of the NFL's official statistics.

A balanced evaluation should consider more than sacks alone:

  • Pass rush: Can he pressure quarterbacks and disrupt passing plays?
  • Run defense: Can he occupy blockers and limit running lanes?
  • Consistency: Does he produce over a full season, rather than only a short stretch?
  • Availability: Can he stay healthy and play regularly?
  • Cost: Does his on-field contribution justify the picks and contract involved?

Based on the available record, Simmons has demonstrated high-level production. Whether the Bears ultimately get enough value from this trade is a question that only future games and seasons can answer.

What does the Simmons trade mean for the Tennessee Titans?

For Tennessee, the reported return provides two future first-round picks and a fourth-round selection. Those picks can help the team add multiple players across different positions as it builds for future seasons.

The downside is immediate: the Titans are losing a highly productive defensive tackle and a prominent team leader. Replacing his pressure, tackling, and presence in the defensive front will not be straightforward.

The long-term value of the move depends on how Tennessee uses its new draft capital. Picks can become productive starters, but draft selections are not guaranteed to turn into players of Simmons' caliber.

In simple terms, Tennessee is exchanging an established star for opportunities to improve several parts of the roster.

How does this trade affect the Chicago Bears and Ryan Poles?

The deal puts the spotlight on Bears general manager Ryan Poles and the team's approach to building a contender. Chicago is committing valuable future draft capital to acquire a player who can potentially help its defense immediately. Reporting on the deal described the move as a major investment in the team's current ambitions.

The upside is clear: Simmons could strengthen the defensive front and give Chicago another experienced playmaker. The risk is also clear: the two first-round picks will no longer be available to draft young players or use in future trades.

For Poles, the trade will ultimately be judged by results, including Simmons' performance, the team's postseason progress, and what Tennessee does with the picks.

What about Caleb Williams' contract?

Caleb Williams is relevant to the wider Bears conversation because the team is building around its quarterback while also investing in the defense. Chicago signed Williams to a four-year rookie contract in July 2024, which included a fifth-year option because he was a first-round pick. In February 2026, NFL.com reported that Poles was planning ahead for the possibility of a future Williams extension.

The Simmons trade does not itself announce a new Caleb Williams contract. The two topics are connected only in the broader sense that Chicago must balance its current roster spending with future financial decisions.

It would be premature to claim that the Simmons deal will prevent or guarantee a Williams extension. That depends on future negotiations, contract structure, the salary cap, and other roster decisions.

Jeffery Simmons vs. Dexter Lawrence: Are they the same type of player?

Simmons and Dexter Lawrence are both high-profile interior defensive linemen, which explains why fans searching for one player may also look up the other. However, they are different players with separate careers and transaction histories.

In April 2026, the Cincinnati Bengals announced that they acquired Dexter Lawrence II from the New York Giants in exchange for the 10th overall pick in the 2026 draft. That is a separate transaction from the reported Simmons trade.

Comparing defensive tackles requires more than comparing sack totals. Analysts also consider run defense, pressures, snap counts, double-teams, role within the defensive scheme, and contract cost. The better fit for a team depends on its needs and how it uses its defensive line.

Is the Jeffery Simmons trade connected to Micah Parsons?

Not directly based on the reporting reviewed for this article. Simmons' reported move is a specific transaction between the Titans and Bears. Micah Parsons is a separate NFL player, and a search for his name alongside trade-related terms does not establish that the two deals are connected.

Readers should distinguish between a confirmed transaction, a reported trade, and general trade speculation. Search interest can rise around several NFL players at once without indicating that their situations are linked.

The NFL trade deadline is reported as November 10, 2026, at 4 p.m. Eastern Time. More roster moves may follow as teams assess their playoff prospects and future draft needs.

  5. Reactions from both teams' coaches and front offices.

Final verdict: Did the Bears pay too much for Jeffery Simmons?

The Bears have reportedly paid a steep price: two first-round picks and a fourth-rounder for Simmons, while also receiving a 2028 fifth-round pick. The trade gives Chicago an established defensive star, but it reduces the team's future draft flexibility.

For Tennessee, the deal creates an opportunity to add talent through the draft, but the team must replace a player who has been central to its defense. Both sides have a clear rationale, and neither outcome can be judged fully on the day the trade is reported.

The bottom line: Simmons' proven production makes him a meaningful addition for Chicago. Whether the move proves worthwhile will depend on his performance with the Bears and how the Titans use the draft picks they receive.
