The Game Awards 2026 Nominees: Full GOTY List, GTA 6 and Voting Details

November 17, 2026 7 min read Nominees Revealed

The Game Awards 2026 nominations have officially been revealed, giving gamers their first look at the titles competing for this year's biggest video game awards. Among the most anticipated announcements is the Game of the Year 2026 nominees list, featuring six major games, including Grand Theft Auto VI, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the biggest stories of this year's nominations, receiving eight nominations across major categories. Other notable contenders include 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, and Resident Evil Requiem.

Hosted by Geoff Keighley, The Game Awards will return on December 10, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, California. The ceremony will celebrate the year's video games, developers, performances, and creative achievements, alongside new game announcements and live entertainment.

In this guide, you'll find the Game Awards 2026 nominees, the complete Game of the Year shortlist, major category highlights, voting information, and how to watch the upcoming ceremony.

Why Is GTA 6 One of the Biggest Stories?

Grand Theft Auto VI has attracted significant attention after receiving eight nominations at The Game Awards 2026. The nominations include Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, Best Action/Adventure, and Best Performance for Manni L. Perez.

Eligibility Note: The nominations are particularly notable because GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19, just two days after the nominee announcement. The game's eligibility comes from the awards' November 20, 2026, commercial release cutoff.

How Does Voting Work at The Game Awards 2026?

Fans can participate in the awards through the official voting process. However, public voting does not determine the winners by itself. According to the official rules, the final results are calculated using a blended voting system:

Voting GroupWeight
Jury Vote90%
Public Fan Vote10%

Fans can visit The Game Awards official website to check the nomination lists and voting information. The official FAQ states that fan voting closes on December 9, 2026, at 6 p.m. Pacific Time.
