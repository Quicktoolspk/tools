The Game Awards 2026 Nominees: Full GOTY List and Voting Guide

November 17, 2026 7 min read By QuickToolsPK Gaming

The nominees for The Game Awards 2026 have officially been announced, and Grand Theft Auto VI is already making headlines. Rockstar Games' upcoming blockbuster leads this year's nominations with eight nominations, including the highly anticipated Game of the Year (GOTY) award.

The 2026 lineup features major titles such as Resident Evil Requiem, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, 007 First Light and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

With the nominations now revealed, fans can vote for their favorite games ahead of the ceremony on December 10, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.

Quick Summary: The Game Awards 2026 nominations are led by Grand Theft Auto VI with 8 nominations. The GOTY shortlist includes GTA 6, 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The ceremony takes place on December 10, 2026.

How Does Game Awards 2026 Voting Work?

Fans can participate in the voting process through The Game Awards' official website. However, public voting does not determine the winners entirely. According to the official rules, the final results use a blended voting system:

International Jury Public Fan Voting
90%
10%

The nomination process is different from the final winner voting. Most nominees are selected by an international jury of more than 100 media and influencer outlets. Each voting outlet submits a confidential ballot listing its top five choices in each category. Geoff Keighley hosts and produces the event but does not vote on nominees or winners.

When Are The Game Awards 2026?

The ceremony takes place on Thursday, December 10, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, California. The opening act starts at 7:30 PM ET / 4:30 PM PT. The show will be available to stream for free across major streaming platforms.

Viewers can expect game announcements, new trailers, musical performances, and appearances from developers and gaming personalities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the Game of the Year nominees for 2026?
The six nominees are Grand Theft Auto VI, 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
Which game has the most nominations at The Game Awards 2026?
Grand Theft Auto VI leads the nominations with eight, including Game of the Year and Best Game Direction.
How can I vote for Game Awards 2026?
Fans can vote through the official website at TheGameAwards.com. Voting closes December 9, 2026, at 6 PM PT.
What percentage of Game Awards voting comes from fans?
Public fan voting accounts for 10% of the final result, while the voting jury accounts for 90%.
