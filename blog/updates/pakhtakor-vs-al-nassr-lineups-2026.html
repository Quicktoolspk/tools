Pakhtakor vs Al-Nassr Lineups: Confirmed Starting XI & 2–6 Match Result

Monday, November 2, 2026 6 min read Full Time

Al-Nassr delivered an emphatic away performance against Pakhtakor in the AFC Champions League Elite, scoring six goals in a 2–6 victory in Tashkent. João Félix scored twice, while Abdullah Al-Hamdan, Abdulelah Al-Amri, Abdulrahman Ghareeb and Ângelo also found the net.

For fans searching for the Pakhtakor vs Al-Nassr lineups, this match featured Al-Nassr in a 4-4-1-1 formation and Pakhtakor in a 4-2-3-1 setup. Cristiano Ronaldo was not included in Al-Nassr's match squad.

Why Was Cristiano Ronaldo Not Playing Against Pakhtakor?

Cristiano Ronaldo was absent from Al-Nassr's travelling squad for the match against Pakhtakor.

Pre-match reporting said that Ronaldo, Kingsley Coman and Mohamed Simakan were left out as the club managed its players during a busy schedule. The available reporting described the decision as rest and squad management rather than confirming an injury to Ronaldo.

This meant João Félix, Sadio Mané and Abdullah Al-Hamdan carried much of Al-Nassr's attacking responsibility in Tashkent.

Pakhtakor vs Al-Nassr Match Result: 2–6

Al-Nassr took control early and scored three goals in the first half. Pakhtakor managed to respond through Flamarion, but the Saudi side continued its attacking pressure after the break.
