Le Havre vs PSG: Full-Time Result, Goals, Lineups & Match Statistics

Friday, October 30, 2026 6 min read Full Time

PSG defeat Le Havre 5-1 as Achraf Hakimi scores a hat-trick.

Paris Saint-Germain delivered a dominant performance against Le Havre, securing a convincing 5-1 victory in their Ligue 1 Matchweek 9 encounter on Friday, October 30, 2026.

The match took place at Stade Océane in Le Havre, where the home side initially surprised PSG by taking an early lead. However, Paris Saint-Germain responded strongly, scoring five goals and controlling much of the match.

Achraf Hakimi was the standout performer of the evening. The PSG defender scored three goals and provided two assists, playing a decisive role in the visitors' impressive comeback.

The result strengthened PSG's position near the top of the Ligue 1 table, while Le Havre suffered another difficult defeat.

Le Havre vs PSG: All Goals and Scorers

PSG scored five goals after falling behind in the first half. Achraf Hakimi led the comeback with three goals, while Mika Godts and Senny Mayulu also found the net.

Josh Maja opened the scoring for Le Havre, assisted by Rayan Fofana. PSG equalised through Mika Godts, who received a pass from Hakimi.

Hakimi then scored his first goal of the match in the 28th minute, with Senny Mayulu providing the assist. After half-time, Hakimi added two more goals, completing his hat-trick. Mayulu scored PSG's fourth goal with an assist from Hakimi.

Achraf Hakimi's Outstanding Performance

Achraf Hakimi was undoubtedly the star of the match.

The PSG right-back contributed directly to all five of his team's goals, scoring three times and setting up two more. His attacking movement, pace and ability to create opportunities caused constant problems for Le Havre's defence.

Man of the Match: Hakimi's goals came in the 28th, 55th and 69th minutes, and it was his first career hat-trick. His performance made the match particularly memorable and helped PSG secure a comfortable away victory.
