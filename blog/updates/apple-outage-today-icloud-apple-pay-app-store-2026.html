Apple Outage Today: iCloud, Apple Pay, App Store and Other Services Face Disruptions

November 17, 2026 5 min read By QuickToolsPK Tech

Apple users are reporting problems accessing several services on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. The disruption has affected services including iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store, Apple Music and Apple Support, with users experiencing sign-in failures, connection errors and difficulties completing certain transactions.

The situation has developed throughout the day. Apple’s System Status page initially showed limited information, but later updates listed issues affecting multiple services, including iCloud account sign-in and the App Store. The full impact and cause of the disruption have not yet been established.

If you're searching for “Is Apple down?”, here's what is known so far, which services are affected and what you can do if your Apple account or device is not working.

Apple Outage Today: What Happened?

On Tuesday morning in the United States, users began reporting problems with Apple services. Reports increased around late morning and early afternoon, with thousands of complaints appearing across outage-tracking platforms.

The disruption was not limited to one application. Users reported trouble accessing Apple accounts, downloading apps, using Apple Pay and connecting to iCloud.

Apple's official System Status page subsequently listed several service issues. However, outage reports from users and Apple's confirmed service notifications are not always identical.

Is iCloud Down or Is Apple Account Login Not Working?

iCloud and Apple Account sign-in problems are among the major issues reported during this disruption. Affected users have described:

  • Unexpected sign-outs on iPhones and Macs.
  • Difficulty signing into iCloud.
  • Verification failures when entering account credentials.
  • Connection errors when accessing Apple services.
  • Problems accessing files and other cloud-based features.

Apple's System Status information indicated that iCloud Account & Sign In could be slow or unavailable. If you cannot sign into iCloud, avoid repeatedly changing your password. First, check Apple's service status and try again after some time.

How to Check if Apple Services Are Down

If you are experiencing problems with an Apple device, the first step is to check whether Apple has reported an outage.

  • Apple System Status: Official source to check Apple's service availability and reported incidents.
  • Downdetector: View user-submitted reports about Apple, iCloud, Apple Pay and the App Store.

Apple's System Status page is the best source for officially acknowledged service incidents. Downdetector can help identify a sudden increase in user complaints, but its reports do not independently confirm an outage.

What Should You Do If Apple Is Not Working?

If your iPhone, Mac or Apple services are experiencing problems, try these steps:

  1. Check Apple's System Status page: Look for incidents affecting the service you are trying to use.
  2. Check your internet connection: Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to rule out a local connection problem.
  3. Restart the affected application: Close the App Store, Apple Music or other affected application and reopen it.
  4. Avoid unnecessary account changes: Do not reset your Apple Account password unless there is a genuine security or password issue.
  5. Wait and try again: If Apple confirms a service disruption, some features may return as the issue is resolved.
  6. Check payment transactions: If Apple Pay fails, verify your bank transaction history before attempting another payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Apple down today, November 17, 2026?
Apple experienced reported disruptions affecting multiple services on November 17, including iCloud, the App Store, Apple Music and Apple Pay. The exact impact varied by service.
Is Apple Pay down right now?
Users have reported Apple Pay problems during the disruption. Check Apple's System Status page and your bank's transaction history for the latest information.
Why is my Apple Account not letting me sign in?
A wider authentication or iCloud service disruption may prevent some users from signing in. Check Apple's official status page before changing your password.
Is Apple having a cyberattack?
There is no confirmed evidence in the cited reports establishing that a cyberattack caused the November 17 Apple service disruption.

Final Thoughts

The November 17 Apple outage has raised questions about the availability of iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store and other services. For users experiencing login failures, payment problems or app download errors, checking Apple's official System Status page is the best starting point.

The full cause and impact of the disruption remain unclear. This story should be updated as Apple publishes further information and affected services return to normal.
