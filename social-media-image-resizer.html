Why Use QuickToolsPK's Social Media Image Resizer?

Creating content for every platform is time-consuming — each social network demands a different image size, aspect ratio, and resolution. QuickToolsPK's social media image resizer lets you upload one image and instantly generate perfectly sized versions for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Pinterest — all at once.

Unlike most online tools, your images never leave your device. Everything runs securely in your browser, making it one of the most private and fastest image resizers available online.

Supported Platform Sizes

  • Instagram Post — 1080 × 1080 px (1:1)
  • Instagram Story / Reel — 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)
  • Facebook Post — 1200 × 630 px
  • Facebook Cover — 851 × 315 px
  • YouTube Thumbnail — 1280 × 720 px
